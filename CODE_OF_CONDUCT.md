# Code of Conduct

Beam follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/) spirit: be kind, be respectful, assume good intent.

## Our standards

- Use welcoming, inclusive language (English or Arabic — both are welcome).
- Respect differing viewpoints; focus on the technical problem.
- No harassment, insults, or personal attacks. No spam or off-topic promotion.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any project space on GitHub.

## Enforcement

Unacceptable behavior may be removed or blocked by maintainers at their discretion. Reports: open a private security advisory or contact the maintainer via the profile email. All reports stay confidential.
